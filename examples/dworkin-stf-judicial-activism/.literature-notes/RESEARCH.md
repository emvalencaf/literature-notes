# Limits of Judicial Activism at the Brazilian Supreme Court (STF)

## Research Question

What are the doctrinal limits, if any, on judicial activism at Brazil's
Supreme Federal Court (STF) between 2015 and 2023? Sub-question: when does a
decision that goes beyond literal rule-application count as legitimate
principle-weighing rather than unconstrained discretion?

## Objectives

**General**: Map how STF decisions in the period justify departing from a
strict rule-application model, and evaluate that justification against a
rule/principle framework.

**Specific**:
- Identify recurring patterns of principle-weighing invoked by STF opinions.
- Distinguish, case by case, principle-weighing from unjustified discretion
  (see [[Glossary#Decisionismo]]).
- Assess whether Brazilian doctrinal critiques of discretion change how the
  rule/principle framework should be applied locally (see
  [[decisions/2026-09-15--incorporate-streck-critique]]).

## Delimitation

Limited to STF decisions and the doctrinal literature discussing them,
2015-2023. Does not address other Brazilian courts (STJ, TRFs) or
comparative constitutional courts outside Brazil, except where a source
under review builds directly on non-Brazilian theory (e.g. Dworkin) to
critique STF practice.

## Rationale

Judicial activism at the STF is frequently invoked in Brazilian public debate
as either a guarantee of rights or a threat to separation of powers, without a
shared definition of where "activism" ends and "unjustified discretion"
begins. Pinning that boundary down, source by source, is the basis for the
dissertation's own doctrinal position.

## Initial Theoretical Framework

Starts from [[dworkin-1986]]'s rule/principle distinction
([[Glossary#Judicial Activism]]) as the baseline model for legitimate
departure from rule-application. Broadened, following the rescope interview
of 2026-09-15, to incorporate [[streck-2014]]'s critique that a
principle-weighing framework can itself collapse into
[[Glossary#Decisionismo]] when the weighing isn't reconstructible from the
opinion's own reasoning — see
[[decisions/2026-09-15--incorporate-streck-critique]] for the full argument.
