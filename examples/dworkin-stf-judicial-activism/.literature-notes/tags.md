# Literature Notes — Tag Vocabulary

Controlled vocabulary for the `tags` field in `.literature-notes/sources/*.md`
and `.literature-notes/notes/*.md`. When the literature-notes skill finds a
concept that doesn't fit an existing tag, it proposes a new entry here instead
of inventing a free-form one — confirm before it's added.

Format: `- <tag-slug> — <short description>`

<!-- tags go below this line -->
- regra-vs-principio — Dworkin's distinction between rules (all-or-nothing)
  and principles (weighed against each other).
- ativismo-judicial — courts deciding beyond the strict application of
  existing rules, substituting their own policy judgment.
- decisionismo — a ruling justified mainly by the judge's own conviction
  rather than by a reconstructible legal argument.
- discricionariedade-judicial — the space judges have to choose among more
  than one legally defensible outcome.
